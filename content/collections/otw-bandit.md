Notes and solutions from my journey through OverTheWire's Bandit wargame.

A progressively structured set of Linux and security challenges, Bandit
provides a simple environment for learning how to investigate unfamiliar
systems, recognize patterns, manipulate data, and understand the tools
available from the command line.

This collection follows the challenges in order, documenting the reasoning,
techniques, and discoveries behind each level rather than simply recording
the final answers.
